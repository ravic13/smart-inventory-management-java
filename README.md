📦 Smart Inventory Management SystemA robust, Java-based desktop application designed for efficient stock control, product tracking, sales management, and business operations. Built with a modular architecture and powered by a relational database via JDBC, this system helps businesses seamlessly manage their entire supply chain workflow.✨ Key FeaturesSecure Authentication (Login.java): Role-based access control ensuring secure navigation and system entry.   Centralized Dashboard (Dashboard.java): Provides a comprehensive overview of core inventory metrics, activities, and quick-access navigation.   Inventory & Product Management (InventoryPage.java, ProductsPage.java): Real-time stock level monitoring, product addition, and detail updates.   Sales & Purchase Orders (SalesPage.java, PurchaseOrdersPage.java): Efficient tracking of outgoing sales transactions and incoming stock purchase orders for automated replenishment.   Supplier Management (SuppliersPage.java): Vendor and supplier database tracking to optimize procurement pipelines.   Business Analytics & Reporting (ReportsPage.java): Generates insightful stock and business reports to assist with data-driven decision-making.   🛠️ Technology StackLanguage: JavaDatabase Connectivity: JDBC (mysql-connector-j)   UI & Architecture: Custom Swing/AWT components managed with dedicated UI helper utilities   🚀 Getting StartedPrerequisitesJava Development Kit (JDK) installed on your machine.A relational database management system (like MySQL).An IDE compatible with Java (e.g., NetBeans, IntelliJ IDEA, or Eclipse).
